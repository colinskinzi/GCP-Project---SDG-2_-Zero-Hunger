# GCP-Project---SDG-2_-Zero-Hunger
📊 Advancing SDG 2: Zero Hunger through SME Empowerment &amp; Smart Policy

This capstone project analyzes how SME income growth and targeted government support can drive agricultural productivity and accelerate progress toward SDG 2: Zero Hunger across African countries. Using cross-country data, the study examines income–productivity dynamics, gender income disparities, and the effectiveness of public agricultural spending through the Agricultural Orientation Index (AOI).

Key findings show a strong positive correlation between SME income and productivity, persistent gender-based income gaps, and evidence that government support is often inefficient when poorly targeted. Policy simulations demonstrate a synergy effect, where combining income growth with well-targeted agricultural investment produces significantly higher productivity gains than isolated interventions.

The project concludes with actionable policy recommendations focused on integrated support, equity-driven interventions, and improving the quality—not just the quantity—of agricultural spending to unlock sustainable food security outcomes.
